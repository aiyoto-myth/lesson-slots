Who's coming, and when can they?

Paste rows from your Google Form or spreadsheet (copy the cells, paste here), or type one student per line: Name: the times they gave you. Same last name = siblings.

What can families write?

Things like Mon 3-5, M/W/F 3:30-6pm, Tuesdays 4pm or later, any weekday after 4 except Fri, Thu 15:30-17:00, Sat morning, anytime, Mon 3-6 but not 4-5. A single time like Tue 4:15 means "can start at 4:15". Lesson length: add 45 min or 1 hr anywhere on the line. A preferred time: prefer Thu 5. Siblings with different last names: add family: Lee.

From a Google Form: open the responses spreadsheet, select the rows including the header row, copy, paste. Columns with a day in the header (for example a checkbox grid "Times [Monday]") are read as that day. One row with "Emma & Leo Chen" becomes two students.
